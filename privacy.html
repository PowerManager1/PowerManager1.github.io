Privacy Policy

Black Water Limited Liability Company, doing business as Black Water Industries ("Black Water", "we", "us")

Effective date: 6 November 2026

Black Water provides N.O.L.A.N. and related services. This policy explains how we handle personal data through N.O.L.A.N. and blackwater-industry.com.

Information we collect. We collect contact details such as name, email address and mobile number. We also collect information our customers enter when they use N.O.L.A.N., and basic technical data from visits to our website. If you exchange text messages with us, we collect those messages and the phone numbers used to send or receive them.

How we use it. We use personal data to provide and support N.O.L.A.N., including emergency and safety communications. We also use it to keep the service secure and to meet our legal obligations. We do not use personal data for profiling or advertising.

No sale and no marketing. We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it for targeted advertising. Mobile numbers and text messaging consent data are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Who we share it with. We share personal data with the customer organization running the operation. We also share it with service providers that help us operate, under contracts that limit their use to providing the service. We disclose it when the law requires it, or when we believe in good faith that disclosure is needed to prevent death or serious injury.

How long we keep it. We keep personal data only as long as needed for the operation it relates to, for review of that operation, and to meet legal requirements. We then delete it.

Security. We protect personal data with encryption, access controls and monitoring. If a breach affects your personal data, we notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

Children. N.O.L.A.N. is not directed to children. Information about a minor supplied during an emergency is used only to respond to that emergency.

Your rights. You may ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to opt out of any processing the law lets you refuse. You may also appeal our decision. Email info@blackwater-industry.com with "Privacy request" in the subject, or call (601) 524-2555. We respond within 45 days and will not treat you differently for making a request.

Contact. Black Water Industries, 4780 N I-55 Ste 100-1009, Jackson, MS 39211. info@blackwater-industry.com. (601) 524-2555.

Changes. We post any change to this policy on this page and update the effective date.
