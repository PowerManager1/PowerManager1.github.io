Privacy Policy

Black Water Limited Liability Company, doing business as Black Water Industries ("Black Water", "we", "us")

Effective date: 24 November 2026

Black Water is developing emergency response technology from Jackson, Mississippi. N.O.L.A.N.™ is the AI backbone being developed to collaborate with the mission team. Black Water is not licensing N.O.L.A.N.™, and this website does not provide N.O.L.A.N.™ as a service to customers. This policy describes personal data this website actually collects.

Information we collect. The briefing form collects your name, email address, topic and message. Organization and role are optional. The form does not ask for a phone number. A hidden field is checked only to reject automated spam and is not used as contact information. Our website host may record standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time of the request, to operate and secure the site. We do not use analytics cookies or advertising pixels. This website does not collect text messages or SMS consent. If a text exchange happens outside this website, the SMS Terms describe it.

How we use it. We use briefing details to read your inquiry and reply. We do not use them to provide N.O.L.A.N.™ as a service, and we do not use them for profiling or advertising.

No sale and no marketing. We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it for targeted advertising or marketing.

Who we share it with. We do not share briefing requests with a customer organization. If the form endpoint in our site configuration is set, that form provider receives the submission only to deliver it to us. If it is not set, the form opens your email application and the message is sent from your account. We disclose information when the law requires it, or when we believe in good faith that disclosure is needed to prevent death or serious injury.

How long we keep it. We keep briefing messages only as long as needed to respond and to meet legal requirements, then delete them. Host logs follow the host's retention schedule, which we do not control.

Security. Pages and form submissions are sent over HTTPS. This website does not run its own database. A form provider, when configured, handles the submission under its own security practices.

Children. This website is not directed to children, and the briefing form is not meant for them.

Your rights. You may ask to access, correct or delete personal data you sent us, or to opt out of any processing the law lets you refuse. You may also appeal our decision. Email info@blackwater-industry.com with "Privacy request" in the subject, or call (601) 524-2555. We respond within 45 days and will not treat you differently for making a request.

Contact. Black Water Industries, 4780 N I-55 Ste 100-1009, Jackson, MS 39211. info@blackwater-industry.com. (601) 524-2555.

Changes. We post any change to this policy on this page and update the effective date.
