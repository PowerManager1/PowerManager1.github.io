Privacy Policy: N.O.L.A.N. Emergency Text Messages

Black Water Limited Liability Company, DBA Black Water Industries

Effective date: [Effective date set on the day of publication]

This policy covers the mobile numbers, messages and location replies we handle when N.O.L.A.N. sends emergency text messages during an active search and rescue mission. We send texts to U.S. mobile numbers only.

This is not 911. These texts come from a search team during an active mission. Nobody watches this number the way a 911 center does. If you are hurt, in danger or lost, call 911 first, or text 911 where your area supports it.

Who is responsible. N.O.L.A.N. is software that Black Water Industries provides to public safety agencies and search and rescue organizations. Each mission is run by one of these organizations through its named, authorized users, called Scene Commanders. The organization decides who is texted and why. Black Water Industries runs the software and the messaging for that organization and keeps the records described here.

Who gets these texts. Texts go out only during an active mission, to two groups:

  • The person being searched for. A Scene Commander may text that person's own mobile number because their health or safety is at risk. The texts ask the person to call 911 or share a location. A person in this situation cannot sign up in advance, so no sign-up is needed.
  • Search team members. People on a search team roster who agreed to receive N.O.L.A.N. mission alerts. They get urgent alerts about missions they are assigned to.

What we collect.

  • The mobile number.
  • For a person being searched for, the description the Scene Commander enters. It may include physical or health details needed to find them.
  • The messages we send and their delivery records.
  • Any reply you send, including STOP or HELP.
  • Your location, if you open the location link and allow your phone to share it: latitude, longitude, accuracy estimate and time. We do not track your phone in the background, and we do not get location from your carrier.

How we use it. Only for the active mission:

  • to ask the person being searched for to call 911 or share a location
  • to support and coordinate the search and response for that mission
  • to let the Scene Commander text you back if you reply
  • to send urgent alerts to the search team
  • to say the mission has ended

Location and replies go to the people running that mission: the Scene Commander, SAR Command and the responders they send to help.

Sensitive data. Precise location and health details are sensitive. We use them only to find and help you during the mission. We never use them to build profiles or for any other purpose.

Children. A missing person may be a child. We send no marketing to anyone, and we collect only what an emergency response needs. We do not knowingly collect more from a child than that, and we use it only to find and help the child. A parent or guardian can make any request in this policy for their child.

No sale and no marketing. We do not sell your personal data or share it for targeted advertising. We do not share mobile numbers, location or message data with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. We share data only with:

  • the agency or organization running the mission and its responders
  • our messaging provider, AWS End User Messaging, and the wireless carriers, to deliver texts
  • our hosting providers, who may use it only to provide the service

Legal requests and emergencies. We disclose information when a valid subpoena, court order, warrant or other legal process requires it. We also disclose it to 911 centers, law enforcement or other responders when we believe in good faith that this is needed to prevent death or serious injury. In each case we disclose only the minimum needed.

How long we keep it.

  • Delivery records (number texted, time, message ID, delivery status, and who entered the number for which mission) are kept for 4 years after the mission closes. Message content is not part of these records.
  • Location replies, text replies and descriptions are kept up to 90 days after the mission stands down, then deleted or de-identified. The organization that ran the mission may export its mission data before then.
  • The do-not-text list. If you reply STOP, your number stays on it for as long as the service runs, so we never text it again unless you text START.

How we protect it.

  • Access is limited by role to the mission's command team and the Black Water Industries staff and systems that run N.O.L.A.N.
  • Our messaging provider, AWS End User Messaging, encrypts message data in transit and at rest within AWS. Once a text leaves AWS for your phone, it travels over the standard SMS network.
  • If a breach affects your personal data, we tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

Your choices.

  • Stopping texts. Reply STOP to any message (QUIT, END, REVOKE, OPT OUT, CANCEL and UNSUBSCRIBE also work), or email sar-ops@blackwater-industry.com, or call (601) 524-2555, and we stop texting your number.
  • Location. Sharing location is your choice, and the location page asks for your consent before anything is sent. You can deny permission or close the page at any time.
  • Your data. To see, correct or delete your data, email info@blackwater-industry.com with "Privacy request" in the subject and the mobile number concerned. We answer within 45 days. If you disagree with our answer, reply to ask for a review (appeal), and we answer that within 60 days. If you still disagree, you can contact your state attorney general. We never treat anyone differently for making a request. A stopped number stays on the do-not-text list after a deletion request, because that list keeps you from being texted again.
