Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 November 2026

Who we are

This policy describes how Black Water Limited Liability Company, DBA Black Water Industries, 4780 N I-55 Ste 100-1009, Jackson, MS 39211, handles personal information on blackwater-industry.com.

It covers this website. Text messages, if you exchange them with us, are covered by our SMS Terms.

Information the site collects

The pages do not include analytics scripts, advertising tags, or code that sets cookies or writes to browser storage.

If you use the briefing form, your browser opens an email to info@blackwater-industry.com containing the name, organization, role, email, topic, and message you entered. We receive that email. If your browser does not open an email, the form does not send the information to us.

You can also write to info@blackwater-industry.com or call (601) 524-2555. We then have the information you choose to send.

The host that serves these pages may keep ordinary technical logs, such as IP address, date and time, and the address of the page requested, in order to operate the service. This site's code does not read those logs.

How we use it

We use briefing requests and other messages you send us to answer you, to arrange a briefing, and to keep a record of that correspondence.

We do not use this website for advertising profiles, and we do not sell personal information.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

The email service that hosts info@blackwater-industry.com processes messages so they can be delivered to and stored for us.

We disclose information when the law requires it, or when we believe in good faith that disclosure is needed to prevent death or serious injury.

Retention

We keep correspondence for as long as we need it to respond, to keep ordinary business records, and to meet legal duties. We then delete it.

Security

Access to the company mailbox is limited to people who need it to respond. Email can be read while it is in transit if the path is not protected, so do not include information you do not want sent by email.

Your rights, including U.S. state privacy rights

You may ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you from your messages to this site, to correct it, or to delete it. You may appeal a decision. You may opt out of sale or sharing for cross-context behavioral advertising; we do not sell or share personal information that way.

These requests cover rights available under the laws of U.S. states that provide them, including California. We will not treat you differently for making a request.

Email info@blackwater-industry.com with the subject Privacy request, or call (601) 524-2555. We respond within 45 days.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them through the site. If you believe a child sent us personal information through the briefing form or by email, contact us and we will delete it.

Text messages

Our SMS Terms describe text messages Black Water may send or receive. This website does not include a text-message signup form.

Changes

We post changes on this page and change the effective date.
