About Black Water Industries

A different approach to protecting the people who protect others

Black Water Industries was founded in Jackson, Mississippi, in January 2026 by Dr Robert J. Di Franco, DDes, following an experience that changed how he thought about technology in emergency operations.

Today, we're developing proprietary artificial intelligence and autonomous systems for search and rescue, public safety and hazardous environments, guided by a belief that the people putting their lives at risk deserve better tools and information.

Discover our technology

Our story began in Ukraine

While in Ukraine, our founder spent time alongside drone operators and the crews manufacturing replacement parts using 3D printing in the field. He saw how quickly they adapted their equipment to meet immediate needs, often working with separate AI tools and systems that had to be brought together under considerable pressure.

The ingenuity was remarkable, but so was the amount of effort required to make everything work together.

That experience stayed with him.

Dr Di Franco recognized a similar challenge facing emergency services in the United States. Firefighters, paramedics, search and rescue teams and law enforcement officers regularly enter dangerous environments, yet the technologies available to them don't always communicate effectively.

He founded Black Water to explore a better approach, bringing the intelligence behind an operation and the physical assets supporting it into a common framework.

Meet our founder

Dr Robert J. Di Franco, DDes

Founder & Principal

Dr Di Franco brings two decades of experience leading multibillion-dollar giga-projects across Europe, the Gulf and international markets, working with sovereign, institutional and private owners.

His career has focused on managing difficult operations, coordinating specialist teams and delivering major projects where decisions carry significant consequences.

He also holds a Doctor of Design and developed A.E.M.T.™ (AI-Enhanced Modular Thinking), a design philosophy shaped by his professional experience and doctoral research.

Inspired by Christopher Alexander's approach to structured design, A.E.M.T.™ explores how modular thinking and AI collaboration can inform the development of complex systems.

That philosophy now guides the development of Black Water's N.O.L.A.N.™ virtual AI and Kannon™ physical AI technologies.

Where we're heading

Our ambition is to make autonomous technology a practical part of emergency operations, giving responders a better understanding of hazardous environments before they enter.

As Black Water develops its technology and manufacturing capabilities, that purpose will continue to guide our work.
