Why Black Water exists

In Ukraine, Dr Di Franco worked alongside drone operators and field 3D-printing teams. He watched crews stitch together separate AI tools on different platforms under pressure, and he saw the same gap in U.S. search and rescue, fire, EMS and police teams. Black Water was founded in 2026 to close it.

The thinking behind Black Water

Dr Di Franco is the creator of A.E.M.T.™ (AI-Enhanced Modular Thinking), a framework he developed over two decades of giga-project delivery and the focus of his Doctor of Design research at the University of Hertfordshire. Building on Christopher Alexander's approach to structured design thinking, A.E.M.T.™ pairs it with AI and proven modern technologies to treat modularity as a way of thinking, with AI as a collaborator rather than a calculator.
