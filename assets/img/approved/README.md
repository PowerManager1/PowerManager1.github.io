# Approved marks, 5 Nov 2026
Robert approved these exactly as shown on 5 Nov 2026 (relayed by Branding). The SVGs are byte-for-byte copies of their sources, renamed only. The PNGs are headless Chrome renders of those SVGs: 512 px wide, with an alpha channel. Icons are square renders of the symbol. Each favicon.ico holds 16, 32 and 48 px.

Caveats
- The N.O.L.A.N. symbol SVGs, and the L.I.F.E. dark lockup and dark symbol SVGs, include their own full-bleed background rectangle (Carbon or Bone). Their PNGs are therefore not transparent. Removing that rectangle would change the artwork, so I left it.
- The source SVGs for N.O.L.A.N. b and Kannon r5-c contain an XML comment that says "DRAFT". It is not visible in the artwork and was left verbatim.
- The L.I.F.E. SVGs use live <text> set in Barlow Condensed. The PNGs are correct, but a browser without that font will fall back to another font. For web use, prefer the PNGs or outline the text later (with approval).
- The N.O.L.A.N. lockups come from nolan-redcore/lockups (the variant b red core applied to the existing lockup).
