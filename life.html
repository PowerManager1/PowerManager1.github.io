L.I.F.E.™

Getting essential supplies to people when help can't reach them

When someone is stranded by rising floodwaters or trapped in a remote location, getting essential supplies to them is hard. Rescue teams may know exactly where someone is, yet dangerous conditions can prevent them from reaching that person.

Black Water Industries is developing L.I.F.E.™, a proprietary system designed to deliver critical supplies to people who cannot be reached safely or immediately.

Because help can't always wait

L.I.F.E.™ is being developed with one clear purpose: to help bridge the gap between someone needing assistance and responders being able to reach them.
