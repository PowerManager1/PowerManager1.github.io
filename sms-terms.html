This is not 911. N.O.L.A.N. Alerts does not replace 911 and does not connect you to 911. If you are in danger, call 911, or text 911 where your area supports it.

Program. Black Water Industries N.O.L.A.N. Alerts sends emergency text messages during active search and rescue missions run by public safety agencies and search and rescue organizations through their authorized Scene Commanders. We send to U.S. mobile numbers only. There are two kinds of messages:

  1. Messages to the person being searched for. These go out because their health or safety is at risk. They ask the person to call 911 or share a location.
  2. Mission alerts to search team members who agreed to receive them when they joined a team roster.

No marketing is ever sent.

How often. A person being searched for gets:

  • one first message
  • up to 3 automatic follow-ups, at least 30 minutes apart, while the mission is active
  • one message when the mission ends

Automatic follow-ups stop when you reply, text STOP, or the mission ends. If you reply, the Scene Commander may text you back. Team members get alerts only while a mission they are assigned to is active. Message frequency for team members varies by mission.

Cost. Message and data rates may apply.

Stop. Reply STOP to opt out. QUIT, END, REVOKE, OPT OUT, CANCEL and UNSUBSCRIBE also work, and so does any reply that plainly asks us to stop. You can also email sar-ops@blackwater-industry.com or call (601) 524-2555. We stop texting your number at once and send one confirmation text with nothing else in it. After that, no N.O.L.A.N. Alerts texts go to your number from any mission unless you text START.

Help. Reply HELP for help, or email sar-ops@blackwater-industry.com (24/7). You can also call (601) 524-2555, [PENDING: phone support hours]. For general questions, email info@blackwater-industry.com.

Location. Our text may include a link on blackwater-industry.com. If you open it, your phone asks whether to share your location with the search team. Nothing is shared unless you agree on your phone. You can say no, close the page or turn off location at any time. The link stops working when the mission ends or after you text STOP. With no data signal or with location services off, the link may not work. In that case, call 911 and describe where you are.

Delivery. Texts depend on your phone, your carrier, signal, terrain and weather. Neither we nor the carriers can promise that a text will arrive, or arrive on time. Wireless carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. To the extent the law allows, Black Water Industries is not responsible for a delayed or undelivered text. Nothing in these terms limits a right the law gives you that cannot be waived.

Team member sign-up. Team members join by entering their mobile number on their team's roster form and checking an unchecked box that reads: "I agree to receive N.O.L.A.N. Alerts mission texts from Black Water Industries at this number. Msg frequency varies. Msg and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to cancel. Consent is not a condition of team membership."

Privacy. We do not sell or share mobile numbers, location or message data with third parties for marketing. See our Privacy Policy at https://blackwater-industry.com/privacy.

Governing law. Mississippi law governs these terms, and federal law governs where it applies. Nothing here limits any right you have under the law of the state where you live. To the extent the law allows, any dispute will be heard in the courts of Hinds County, Mississippi, or the U.S. District Court for the Southern District of Mississippi.
